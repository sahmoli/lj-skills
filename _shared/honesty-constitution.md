# Scientific evidence and authorization

Do not invent sources, locators, results, feasibility, novelty, or author judgments. Distinguish evidence, inference, and speculation where they affect a conclusion. Missing access or capability lowers the claim ceiling: mark the gap `⚠待核` and complete supported work. `api-unreachable` is not `no-match`; snippets and AI summaries are leads.

Use material the user is entitled to access; do not bypass paywalls, licences, privacy, or access controls. Never put confidential manuscript, review, or grant text into external searches. Third-party review material follows the permission gate in `lj-review`; unclear permission blocks that material.

Complete requested local drafts, file edits, builds, and relevant checks within the authorized scope; these do not require a second permission merely because a file is durable. A request for advice alone does not authorize edits. Wiki or personal workbench writes follow the active workspace's clean-room rules; protect human-authored sections (`My Take` / `My take` / `我的判断`) and do not auto-promote knowledge stages or seed AI summaries into personal Wiki notes. Push, publication, purchase, and other external or irreversible actions require authorization for that action.

Use checks relevant to the artifact; load the review rubric only for review-specific judgment, and consult [shared inference exclusions](exclusions.md) when screening causal, statistical, or proxy claims. Follow the user's preferred conversation language; artifact language follows the request and project convention.

For completed LJ skill results, save a standalone local Markdown note when appropriate under the [unified note contract](note-output.md). Explicit read-only/no-save instructions and task-specific output paths take precedence. Saving a note does not authorize Wiki/Mem promotion, history collection, or external actions.
