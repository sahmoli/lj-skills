# Journal-led landscape and opportunity scans

Read for requests such as “还有哪些跨学科期刊值得看”, “近两年这些刊发表了什么”, or
“我的现有研究怎样接入这些刊”. Keep a narrow fit check narrow.

## Frame the search

Establish the research question or portfolio, intended use (reading, present submission, or future
development), constraints and date window. Resolve “past two years” to explicit endpoints using the
user's current date. Do not permanently reuse the 2024-10-20–2026-10-20 window of the seed material.
State reasonable assumptions and proceed when the request supplies enough context.

Use current local project summaries when personalization depends on project status. Historical notes,
public webpages and another AI's description are attributed context, not confirmation of available data,
completed analyses or active priorities. Read `CURRENT.md` where relevant; do not inspect entire private
projects or transmit their contents merely to make a venue recommendation.

Search across disciplinary problems rather than only “biodiversity” and familiar ecology titles: urban
development, production and consumption, climate adaptation, governance, behaviour, health, measurement
and inference. The entry map is a set of starting hypotheses; add or discard venues as the evidence
warrants. Do not require a fixed number of journals or papers.

## Build a traceable paper sample

Use publisher scope/content-type pages for remit and rules, and accessible original articles for research
content. Search snippets and AI summaries discover leads; they do not establish methods or results. When
only an official index/abstract or an author manuscript is accessible, identify that access level and
limit the claim accordingly. An access failure is neither an empty literature nor a reason to invent
fields. Record consequential gaps and continue supported parts.

For each paper that supports a recommendation, retain enough to recover the evidence:

| Field | What to preserve |
|---|---|
| Identity | Full original title, journal, DOI and original URL; deduplicate DOI/version variants |
| Time | First-online date when verified; otherwise issue month/year at its actual precision; retrieval date separately |
| Type | Publisher article type **and** study design: Article may contain a meta-analysis; Analysis or Resource may contain original work |
| Relevance | Direct biodiversity/conservation, adjacent-domain precedent, or conceptual/viewpoint entry |
| Contribution | Question, measured/modelled outcome, main comparison and supportable finding |
| Transfer | Why this matters to that journal's readership; the proposed bridge to the user's question, explicitly an inference |
| Boundary | Access level, limitations affecting the transfer, and verification status/source locator |

Check corrections, retractions and updated versions when they affect the cited claim. Do not silently
reuse a superseded number. A licensing/Crossmark date is not automatically a publication date. If only
the issue date is known, inclusion at a strict first-online boundary remains provisional.

A representative scan is not a systematic review. State the search window, selection logic and coverage
limits. Sample counts measure this sample only. “No close direct precedent found in this search” does
not mean the journal never publishes the topic or the idea is novel. Keep older foundational references
outside the recent-paper count and label them as such.

## Evaluate the bridge, not the label

For each promising route, connect:

**existing scientific asset → question for the target readership → distinctive contribution → missing
evidence/partner → smallest informative next check.**

Separate three decisions: worthwhile to read now; plausible for the evidence already available; worthwhile
to develop after specific prerequisites. Explain priority through scientific importance, overlap with
existing work, evidence accessibility, incremental burden and the value of the first test. A high-profile
journal is not itself a reason to add variables, collect global data or start another project.

Compare close precedents by question, outcome, design and inferential capability. Identify what a proposed
study could add, rather than calling the use of AI, a new region or a new dataset intrinsically novel.
For methods venues, distinguish a reusable methodological/resource contribution from applying a tool;
compare strong existing methods, meaningful generalization and downstream scientific utility.

Maintain the claim boundary in the transfer: observations versus detection; co-occurrence versus
interaction; greenness/land cover versus animal recovery; modelled exposure/risk versus observed loss;
attention/intentions versus behaviour; commitments/reporting versus independent outcomes; explanatory
model attribution versus causal intervention. These are checks on the proposed inference, not blanket
rejections of modelling, observational studies or conceptual article types.

## Deliver and maintain

Usually give a short priority judgment plus a comparison table with contribution, precedent, missing
evidence and next action. Add a traceable paper ledger when the breadth warrants one or the user asks.
Do not impose a spreadsheet on a small question. Keep scope statements, empirical precedents, personal
context and your recommendations identifiable rather than blending their evidentiary status.

For authorized updates to a research-idea file, map new directions to existing cards, retain stable IDs,
and integrate genuine refinements. Add a card only for a distinct question. Preserve source filenames,
original evidence and earlier judgments; mark newly proposed priorities as provisional. Do not turn a
question into an assumed result or automatically promote it into an active project/Wiki knowledge.

Keep the maintained layers separate:

- Journal cards: verified, sourced journal/publisher facts under the existing schema.
- Entry map: reusable search prompts and contribution judgments, not caps or personal commitments.
- Research notes: project-specific assets, hypotheses, priorities and next tests.

Update only fields actually checked. Imported workbooks can be retained as source snapshots with their
own claimed verification date; never bulk-upgrade those records to `official` on import.
