# Biodiversity beyond ecology journals: candidate entry map

Use selectively for cross-disciplinary discovery. This is a search and reasoning aid, not a journal
ranking, an exhaustive list, or a statement of editorial requirements. Confirm the current remit,
article type and relevant precedents before making a recommendation. Familiar specialist ecology and
conservation journals remain valid comparisons, including when they best serve the current evidence.

## Candidate routes

The contribution questions below are analytical prompts. They are not publisher rules and do not
require every ecological study to become a decision/optimization paper.

| Candidate journal | Question that could make biodiversity central to its readership | Evidence gap to examine |
|---|---|---|
| Nature Cities | How do urban development, land history or management shape ecological outcomes and urban choices? | Actual urban process and comparison, not just an urban sampling location |
| npj Urban Sustainability | Which urban arrangements or interventions improve sustainability under local constraints? | Comparable land-use options, scale and relevant social/environmental outcomes |
| Nature Sustainability | How do production, demographic or institutional transitions change ecological outcomes and trade-offs? | Actual transition/pressure and mechanism; population decline alone is not pressure release |
| One Earth | How do environmental change and social systems interact, and which responses work? | Link claims, actions and independently supported outcomes |
| Global Environmental Change | How do institutions, distribution, power or behaviour shape environmental change? | Social process and appropriate evidence; a generic policy paragraph is insufficient |
| Nature Food | How do production, sourcing or consumption choices change ecological costs of food provision? | Actual product/production/management data and a meaningful comparison unit; threat codes alone cannot supply them |
| Nature Climate Change | How do climate mechanisms or climate responses change biodiversity prospects? | Climate contribution central to the question; distinguish projections from observed effects |
| Communications Earth & Environment | What environmental process or land-system change does the spatial evidence explain? | Validated exposure/outcomes and uncertainty rather than only overlaying maps |
| Nature Communications | What robust, consequential insight does the study add to the relevant research field? | Contribution beyond the local application; no automatic requirement for global extent |
| Environmental Science & Technology | Which environmental stressor, product system or control strategy produces ecological consequences? | Quantified pressures, system boundaries and interpretable intervention/comparison |
| Nature Water | How do water processes, quality or management affect ecological functions and restoration choices? | Water-system evidence; being in a headwater region is not enough |
| Nature Energy | How does ecology change energy-system choices, costs or performance? | Energy demand/output and feasible alternatives; facility overlap alone may not answer this |
| Nature Human Behaviour | What does the study explain about human decisions, norms or behaviour? | Valid behavioural outcomes and mechanism; attention and intentions need separate interpretation |
| The Lancet Planetary Health | What population-health or health-policy question does the environmental evidence resolve? | Relevant exposure/health outcomes and design; a nature-health paper is not automatically biodiversity evidence |
| Nature Methods | What general biological measurement, inference or reusable resource limitation is overcome? | Strong baselines, calibration, reproducibility and scientific usefulness beyond one application |
| Nature Computational Science | What scientific capability follows from a computational advance? | Computational contribution and transferable validation, not merely larger analysis |
| Nature Machine Intelligence | What advances machine learning or machine intelligence itself? | Methodological contribution, uncertainty and generalization beyond one ecological task |
| Science Robotics | What advances autonomous sensing, sampling, control or robotic operation? | Engineering contribution and field performance; using an existing drone is not itself a robotics advance |
