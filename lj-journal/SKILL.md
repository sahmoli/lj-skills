# lj-journal

Match depth to the request: a word-limit lookup checks official article-type rules directly; a manuscript shortlist compares present evidence against candidate venues; a cross-disciplinary venue scan follows [landscape workflow](references/landscape-workflow.md) and [biodiversity entry map](references/biodiversity-entry-map.md). Question generation belongs to `lj-question`, study design to `lj-design`, and manuscript critique to `lj-review`.

## Core Discipline
- **Local cards are navigation aids, never current authority:** inspect relevant `journals/<slug>.yaml`, `publisher_profiles.yaml`, and `journals/_schema.yaml` only for needed fields; missing cards never exclude a venue.
- **Verify decision-changing rules live:** check current official author guidelines per **article type** (Research Article, Letter, Brief Communication, Review, Data Paper), recording source URL, retrieval date, and confidence (`official` / `secondary` / `inferred` / `unknown` / `⚠待核`). Never extrapolate rules across sibling journals.
- **Separate three distinct judgments:** (1) fit of present evidence now, (2) conditional fit requiring substantial new data/analysis, and (3) journal-led reading opportunity. Do not predict acceptance probabilities from prestige or JIF.
- **Metric hygiene:** record metric name, value, metric year, source, and retrieval date separately; do not mix JIF, 5-year JIF, and CiteScore. Treat `my_notes` in journal cards strictly as personal attributed notes rather than universal editorial rules.

Follow the [suite honesty and authorization rules](../_shared/honesty-constitution.md) and [unified Markdown note contract](../_shared/note-output.md). Explicit output paths and read-only/no-save instructions take precedence.
