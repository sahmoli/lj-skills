# lj-question

Choose the requested outcome; these modes are independent:

- **Explore:** a broad, unranked pool. Read [exploration guidance](references/roam-engine.md) when deliberate diversity or cross-domain structure mapping helps; validate saved pool artifacts with [validate_pool.py](scripts/validate_pool.py).
- **Select:** compare supplied candidates by importance, mechanism, discriminating test, feasible evidence, and supported fit. Read [question guidance](references/question-engine.md) for full comparison or operationalization.
- **Explain:** identify competing processes and the testable crux behind an observed pattern, or name the evidence gap that halts the descent. Read [mechanism guidance](references/dig-engine.md) and validate saved dig artifacts with [validate_dig.py](scripts/validate_dig.py).
- **Constraints:** show how a hard, soft, self-imposed, or unknown boundary changes feasibility, with a counterfactual distinguishing it from ordinary difficulty. Read [constraint lenses](references/constraint-lenses.md) and validate saved constraint audits with [validate_constraint.py](scripts/validate_constraint.py).

**30-Second Elevator & Anti-Defensive Gate:** Every recommended question must be restatable in ≤30 seconds (the puzzle, the strongest rival explanation, and the observation that separates them). Calibrate scope to the project tier (`course/undergrad` vs `summer/pilot` vs `PhD/flagship`) — never pad a sharp question with defensive sub-clauses or extra analyses merely to pre-empt hypothetical critiques.

Preserve observation, comparison, unit, scale, and evidence status. A structural analogy needs matching relations, a checkable prediction, and a breakpoint. Novelty requires a precedent check (`lj-evidence`); treat gaps derived from abstracts alone as `hypothesis-level` until key primary full texts are read. Keep unknown feasibility, access, and constraints visible. Exclude unethical-by-construction options.

Follow the [suite honesty and authorization rules](../_shared/honesty-constitution.md) and [unified Markdown note contract](../_shared/note-output.md). Explicit output paths and read-only/no-save instructions take precedence.
