# Selection, Operationalization & Scope Gate (`Select`)

Single source of truth for comparing candidate questions, operationalizing units, and preventing defensive scope creep.

## 1. Six-Criterion Gate (`pass / fail / unknown` — Never Hide `unknown` in a Numeric Score)
1. **Anomaly (`反常`):** Exposes a real mismatch between a default model/assumption and observation (not merely a cute pairing).
2. **Mechanism (`机制`):** States a concrete domain causal process AND names at least one serious rival explanation (`H_alt`: sampling artefact, confound, demography, or competing process).
3. **Falsifiability (`可证伪`):** Identifies a decisive contrast or observation where `H` and `H_alt` predict differently (correlational description alone fails).
4. **Feasibility (`可行`):** Unit, data/access, comparator, replication, and timeline are plausible; unverified live facts stay `⚠待核`.
5. **Consequence (`重要`):** Names which theoretical commitment, management action, or policy target changes if answered.
6. **Fit (`匹配`):** Matches the researcher's comparative advantage, active data/methods, or target programme; if only data convenience fits, state that plainly.

A `fail` on consequence, mechanism, falsifiability, or ethics eliminates or reframes the candidate. Screen causal and statistical premises against [shared inference exclusions](../../_shared/exclusions.md).

## 2. Four-Unit Mismatch Screen
Verify four units separately before approving any question:
1. **Observation / sampling unit** (e.g., camera-trap night, plot, grid cell);
2. **Mechanism-bearing unit** (individual behaviour, pack/group, local population);
3. **Spatial and temporal grain/extent**;
4. **Decision or intervention unit** (protected area, jurisdiction, management cohort).

Large sample size at the wrong unit cannot rescue an unidentifiable mechanism.

## 3. 30-Second Elevator Test & Anti-Defensive Scope Cap
- **30-Second Test:** A colleague outside the narrow sub-specialty must be able to restate the core question, why it matters, and the killer contrast after 30 seconds. If the question requires three nested caveats to state, strip it back to the load-bearing crux.
- **Tiered Complexity Cap:** Match scope to the real vehicle (`undergrad/course project`: 1 clean contrast on accessible data; `summer/pilot`: 1 focused mechanism + 30-minute data probe; `PhD/flagship`: multi-chapter causal chain or generalizable cross-system test). Never add defensive sub-questions just to appease hypothetical reviewers.
- **Problem-Class Lift:** Ask what class of systems the case represents without inflating narrow case wording into unsupported universality.

## 4. Portfolio Ordering (When Comparing Multiple Survivors)
Expose the real trade-off rather than a single opaque score: (a) grounded conventional core, (b) calibrated atypical bridge (Uzzi-balanced), and optionally (c) high-upside probe with a cheap 30-minute kill test (`→ lj-design`).
