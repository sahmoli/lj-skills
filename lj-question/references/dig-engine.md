# Mechanism & Crux Diagnosis (`Explain`)

Use these diagnostic lenses to separate observed patterns from competing generating processes and locate the testable crux (`命门`). Apply only the lenses needed for the focal puzzle — never print empty multi-layer headings as a ritual.

## 1. Pattern vs. Competing Processes (Gould & Lewontin 1979)
One mechanism fitting an observed pattern never proves it is the sole or true cause. Always pit the proposed explanation against serious rivals:
- **Adaptive / behavioural response** vs. **demographic sorting / differential mortality**;
- **Direct anthropogenic or environmental driver** vs. **structural, phylogenetic, or historical constraint**;
- **Biological signal** vs. **measurement, detectability, or spatial-averaging artefact** (`pattern ≠ process`).

## 2. Level of Analysis Discipline (Tinbergen 1963; Marr 1982)
Proximate *how* (**mechanism**, **ontogeny**) and distal *why* (**current function**, **evolutionary history**) answer different questions and can be simultaneously true. Never treat "it evolved to buffer X" (function) as an answer to "what sensory/behavioural rule triggers X?" (mechanism). Stay on one level once anchored.

## 3. Jargon-Free Restatement & Contingent Crux (`命门`)
- **Feynman Restatement:** Restate the proposed mechanism using plain cross-disciplinary nouns and verbs (flow, threshold, variance, encounter rate, energy balance). If stripping field jargon leaves a tautology, halt and report `named, not explained` while keeping valid observations.
- **First Contingent Layer Stop Rule:** Stop explanatory descent at the **first empirically contingent assumption** `X` where:
  1. **Load-bearing:** If `X` fails, the explanation collapses (Alon 2009 `A → cloud → C` real problem);
  2. **Testable:** `X` makes an observable prediction diverging from the strongest rival (`falsifier` / `opposite prediction`).
  Do not dig further into tautological physical necessities. Check contestedness via `lj-evidence` rather than memory (`literature status`: grounded citation vs `⚠待核`).

## 4. Scientific Accessibility Triage
Classify how the crux can be tested and hand off cleanly to `lj-design` when ready:
- `accessible now` — testable immediately with existing authorized data or published syntheses;
- `accessible with named dependency` — feasible once a specific dataset, permit, or field protocol is secured;
- `not yet accessible` — blocked by current observation limits or multi-decade horizons (record honestly as a long-horizon seed or `status: halted` with the exact missing evidence).

Use `scripts/validate_dig.py` only when validating saved structured diagnosis artifacts.
