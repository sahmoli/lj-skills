# Teaching design handoff

Read this only when an external design package governs a new or substantially
restructured teaching deck. It turns an approved teaching design into a usable
production brief without making every ordinary slide request a form.

## Minimum handoff

Record only the information needed to preserve the design:

- **Context and course baseline:** course and audience, relevant prior learning,
  session duration, connection to the actual lab or next decision, and the
  approved reference deck/version whose recurring visual roles should be
  inherited.
- **Learning route and protected functions:** the core understanding, section
  movement, essential explanation, evidence, student action, and feedback that
  must survive production. Mark suggestions or layout choices that may be
  improved without changing the teaching function.
- **Stable page or activity IDs:** on-canvas content, student action, visible
  evidence and its identity, and the transition to the next step. Add the
  prompt, expected response, feedback, or reveal only when the activity needs
  them. Link speaker-note IDs and time estimates to the same IDs when present;
  an estimate is not a classroom trial.
- **Source and asset record:** source plus usable locator, original/reproduction/
  derivative/illustrative identity, actual inspection or verification status,
  permission constraints, and a usable fallback when needed. A temporary web
  citation or inaccessible link is not enough to reproduce a visual.
- **Revision impacts and open gaps:** identify which IDs, notes, feedback,
  sources, or recurring layouts a material change can affect. State missing
  material, unsupported claims, and unperformed checks rather than treating a
  design instruction as completed output.

The execution agent may improve spacing, crop, hierarchy, and page division
when they retain the protected function and scientific identity. Existing task
authorization covers those ordinary production choices. Surface a concrete
conflict when a protected activity, evidence identity, or confirmed course
decision cannot be retained; continue unaffected work. Do not turn a voluntary
activity into a required submission or add an assessment without a brief that
authorizes it.
